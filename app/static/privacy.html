Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to CalAI ("we", "our", or "us"). We provide an AI-powered conversational assistant to help you view, create, and manage your Google Calendar events. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your personal information is handled securely.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you sign in with Google, we receive your Google ID, full name, email address, and profile picture URL.
  • Google Calendar Data: With your permission, CalAI accesses your Google Calendar (event titles, times, attendees, and summaries) solely to answer your questions and perform requested calendar actions.
  • Chat Messages: Messages you exchange with the assistant are saved so you can access your conversation history across sessions.

3. How We Use and Protect Your Data

Your data is used strictly to provide the assistant's services. Specifically:

  • Your Google OAuth tokens are encrypted using AES-128-CBC/Fernet encryption before being stored in our database.
  • We do not sell, rent, or monetize your calendar or personal data to third parties.
  • We do not use your private Google Calendar data to train generalized AI models.

4. Third-Party Services

CalAI integrates with:

  • Google Cloud & Google Calendar API: For authentication and calendar synchronization.
  • Google Gemini AI: Message context is processed by Google Gemini to interpret scheduling requests.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

You can revoke CalAI's access to your Google Calendar at any time via your Google Account Security Settings. You may also contact us to request the deletion of your account and conversation history.
